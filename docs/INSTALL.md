# Installing PocketLedger

PocketLedger is a Progressive Web App. You open its web address once and "install" it, after which it gets its own icon, opens in its own window, and works offline. It's not in any app store, and it doesn't need one.

App address: `https://cakulkar.github.io/ExpenseTracker/` (after the first deploy; see DEPLOY.md).

## Windows (Edge or Chrome)
1. Open the app address in **Microsoft Edge** or **Google Chrome**.
2. Click the **install icon** at the right end of the address bar.
   - In Edge it may be under **⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app**.
3. Click **Install**. PocketLedger now appears in the Start menu and can be pinned to the taskbar.

## Android (Chrome)
1. Open the app address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮ → Install app**. On some phones this is labelled "Add to Home screen".
3. Confirm. The icon appears in your app drawer and home screen.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari, iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or newer)
1. Open the app address in **Safari**. Other iOS browsers can't install web apps on older iOS versions.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Always open PocketLedger from the Home Screen icon. That copy keeps its data more reliably than a Safari tab.

### iPhone/iPad limitations
- **Storage can be cleared.** Safari may clear data for websites that aren't added to the Home Screen and haven't been used for a while. Installing to the Home Screen avoids this, but **keep backups** regardless (Reports & backup → Download encrypted backup).
- **No background sync.** The app syncs when you open it and while it's open, not while it's closed.
- **Separate data.** The Home Screen app and Safari tabs have separate data. Use only the Home Screen app.

## First run

**First person (sets up the household):**
1. Choose **Set up a new household**, enter both first names, and pick who uses this device.
2. Create this device's **6-digit passcode**.
3. Open **Sync & Drive → Connect Google Drive** and sign in with the **shared household Google account**.
4. Save the **recovery key** that appears. Write it down or put it in a password manager, and give your spouse a copy.

**Second person (joins):**
1. Install the app, then choose **Join our household from Google Drive**.
2. Sign in with the same Google account and enter the recovery key.
3. Pick your name and create your own passcode.

Without Drive you can still copy data to another device with **Restore from a backup file**.

## Updating
When a new version is published, the app shows "A new version is ready" → **Update now**. Your data stays.

## Uninstalling
Removing the app also removes its data on that device. Sync first and download an encrypted backup (Reports & backup), especially if any changes haven't synced.
